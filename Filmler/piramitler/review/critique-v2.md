By 18.5 s (`g18.5.jpg`) the line reflows to "Akhet-Khufu'ya yelken açtık." with GÜN 26 under it. For about 0.5 s the two collide.

Move the GÜN 26 stamp down by one caption line (about 40 px at draft size), below the finished caption, or draw it 0.5 s after the caption has settled.

Put the title on a paper band instead of the river, or give it a light paper halo, so the dark letters are not on blue.

It looks like a wireframe someone forgot to fill in. The payoff (white blocks filling the pyramid, 26 s) only works if that rectangle has read as a stone.
- **Smallest fix:** give the block a stone look in the same ink style: a pale limestone fill, two or three chisel hatch lines and a darker bottom edge, on the boat and on the pyramid blocks alike. At Tura, cut a block-sized notch into the cliff edge (where the dashed box is) and hatch the cliff face, so "taş yükledik" shows where the stone came from.

## What it does differently from our videos
1. **Cubes start at half of the cuts** (round 250: at ~126; 500: at ~255; 1000: at ~510; 10: at 7). The first two directions take ~50% of the cuts, the crosswise direction the other ~50%.
2. **CUBES counter** counts only cubes (0 before), "≈" with thousands rounding for big numbers. Ours counts every piece from the first cut.
3. **Pile = tall cone** in front of the object (pieces stack up), ours spreads flat.
4. **Round change = hard cut + ~0.4 s silence** (no slide).
5. **Long rounds**: 500 ≈ 11.5 s, 1000 ≈ 12.5 s (ours 5.8 s and 6.6 s).
6. **Sound**: single chops in round 10, then a dense continuous chopping texture plus a bright high-frequency crackle (5.5–7.5 kHz) while pieces fall. Ours has knife knocks only.
7. Cover = the final frame (1,000 CUTS + cube count), same as ours.

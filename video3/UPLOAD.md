Nearly half of Americans earning $100K+ say they live paycheck to paycheck. A bigger salary doesn't fix it. 💸🧠
#personalfinance #moneytips #psychologyfacts

It's called lifestyle inflation: every raise quietly turns into a bigger car, a nicer apartment, more takeout. And thanks to the hedonic treadmill, your brain adapts in weeks, so you need even more to feel the same.

👉 The fix: save 50% of every raise the day it hits your account. You'll never miss money you never got used to.

📚 Sources: PYMNTS & LendingClub "Paycheck-to-Paycheck" reports · Brickman & Campbell (1971), "Hedonic relativism and planning the good society".

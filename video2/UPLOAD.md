A dress sold MORE at $39 than at $34. Same dress. Higher price. More sales. 🧠💸
#personalfinance #moneytips #psychologyfacts

It's called the left-digit effect: your brain reads prices left to right and anchors on the first digit, so $9.99 feels like $9, not $10.

👉 The fix: round every price UP in your head. $9.99 = $10. Always.

📚 Sources: Anderson & Simester (2003), "Effects of $9 Price Endings on Retail Sales", Quantitative Marketing and Economics · Thomas & Morwitz (2005), "Penny Wise and Pound Foolish: The Left-Digit Effect in Price Cognition", Journal of Consumer Research.

Costco's hot dog + soda has cost $1.50 since 1985. When the CEO said they were losing money, co-founder Jim Sinegal reportedly answered: "If you raise the hot dog, I will kill you." 🌭
#personalfinance #moneytips #psychologyfacts

The hot dog isn't the product. You are. A cheap hot dog makes the whole store feel cheap, so you keep renewing your membership, and membership fees bring in most of Costco's profit. It's called a loss leader.
